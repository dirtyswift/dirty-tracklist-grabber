# Dirty Tracklist Grabber — Product

## Register
brand

## Product purpose
A Chrome extension that copies the visible tracklist of a Spotify or Deezer page to the clipboard, formatted as `Artist - Title` (one track per line). It bridges the gap between **discovering** music on streaming and **buying** it elsewhere.

## Users
Diggers. DJs, crate-diggers, beat-makers, music collectors. People who listen to playlists from selectors they trust (or build their own digs), and who buy on Apple Music, Beatport, Bandcamp, Discogs. They want the whole list in their hands so they can act on it: buy, archive, share, set a wantlist.

They are NOT general productivity users. They are NOT casual music listeners. They speak French as a first language but the global digging scene is English.

## Voice & tone
- Direct. No fluff, no marketing-speak.
- Slang-friendly: "digging", "1 click", "the actual workflow", "shopping list".
- Honest about limits. The extension breaks when Spotify changes its DOM; we acknowledge it.
- Confident, not corporate. Built by a digger, for diggers.
- French is the default (the maker's voice), English available because the digging scene is international.

## Brand
- Maker: **Dirty Swift / DirtyLab**.
- Brand mark: 4 horizontal black bars on orange — an abstract tracklist seen from above. The bars ARE the brand and can recur as a typographic device, a section divider, or a loading visual.
- Brand colors: orange `#FC4501` (committed) + ink `#0F0F0F` + warm paper. No pure white, no pure black.
- Brand voice is anti-SaaS, anti-corporate, but production-grade in craft.

## Anti-references (NEVER look like)
- SaaS landing templates: hero blob, "trusted by N startups", testimonial carousel, identical 3-column icon-grid features.
- Chrome Web Store default aesthetic (gray, sterile, identical to every other extension).
- Music-tech startup gradient look (Spotify-green to purple to blue).
- Notion-orange productivity vibe.
- Squarespace "creative agency" templates.

## Strategic principles
1. **Show the workflow, not features.** The user already knows what they want; show them THEIR flow. Screenshots are evidence, used big and almost unstyled.
2. **The buy moment is honest.** Apple Music searches one track at a time. We don't hide it; we tell the truth and the user respects us for it.
3. **Privacy is shown by behavior, not by a badge.** No "100% private" trust seal. Just the truth: zero network calls, zero storage, the code is open on GitHub.
4. **The brand is the bars.** The four black bars of the logo are the recurring motif. They appear as separators, as a marquee, as a hover trigger.
5. **French first, English on demand.** Default content is FR. EN exists for international diggers via a toggle, not via a separate routed site.

## Out-of-scope
- Tidal, YouTube Music, SoundCloud support (V2+).
- Email capture / newsletter / pricing.
- Dark mode (the brand IS warm + bright; dark would dilute it).
